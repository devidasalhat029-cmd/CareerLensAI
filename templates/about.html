About CareerLens.ai

CareerLens.ai is a smart career and placement guidance platform designed to help students understand their skills, prepare for opportunities, and build a confident career path.

🚀 What is CareerLens.ai?

CareerLens.ai brings career guidance, placement preparation, interview practice, resume building, career analysis and learning support into one student-friendly platform.

Instead of searching for different resources for different career needs, students can use CareerLens.ai as a central place to prepare themselves for their future.

The platform combines modern web technology with AI-powered assistance to make career preparation easier, smarter and more organized.
